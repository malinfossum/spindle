# Spindle v0.5 — plan

Spec of record for v0.5. v0.4 is closed: the deployed app scans a barcode (or
takes one typed), looks it up on MusicBrainz after one consent, fills the empty
fields from a pick-list, and remembers the barcode on the album. I ran the Scan
flow on my phone on 2026-09-18 and it works.

v0.5 is what that phone session taught me. Two of the items are plain fixes,
three are the flow finishing what it starts, and one — staying unlocked —
changes the security model on purpose, so it gets the most words.

**Status:** shipped. PR #7 merged 2026-09-22 as `701499d` and deployed. `curl -I` on the live site shows the four-host `connect-src`, and a real look-up on `720642442524` fills the fields and ends with the cover from the Cover Art Archive. The phone pass ran the same day and passed. Not verified on iOS, and staying that way for now: I do not have access to an iPhone.

**All decisions are made (2026-09-18).** The questions that were open and how
I answered them are in § 0.

---

## 0 — Decisions

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The scanner delivers the number and then waits for a press. Why? | **It should not.** A scan is the press. The look-up runs the moment the digits land; the owned check and the consent gate sit on the same path as before, so nothing is skipped — only the extra tap. |
| 2 | Cover art was ruled out in v0.4 § 0 #3 as a second host. Now? | **Yes, from the barcode, one image per pick.** The Cover Art Archive is the same project as MusicBrainz and serves a front cover per release group. Photographing the sleeve and "recognising" it online is not on the table: that is image recognition, which means uploading the photo to a vision service — the opposite of this app. The camera on the cover field stays as it is for the albums the archive does not have. |
| 3 | When is the user asked whether look-ups are wanted? | **Once, right after the library is created**, and **no means no**: with look-ups off the app never asks again — Scan still fills the number, the look-up step says look-ups are off and where to turn them on. Asking the same question twice reads as nagging. People who created their library before v0.5 keep the v0.4 dialog on their first press. |
| 4 | Switching apps or letting the screen go dark sends me to Login. Is that a lock? | **No — there is no lock.** Android discards the background tab, the page reloads, and the key only ever lived in memory. So "stay logged in" means keeping a key across reloads, opt-in, and that is a deliberate hole in *nothing readable survives a reload*. Ruled: **an unchecked checkbox on Login, "Stay unlocked on this device", kept until log out.** A fixed window (7 days) would be safer on paper but harder to explain; session-only storage is exactly what Android does not reliably restore, which is the problem being fixed. |
| 5 | Two small chrome bugs from the phone. | **Fix both in the router:** choosing a page closes the mobile menu, and a page change starts at the top. |

A standing rule, stated once here and applied to every item below: **every way
a step can fail gets its own visible message**, in the live region the form
already has, in both languages. A spinner that stops is not a message.

## 1 — The router fixes

`Controller/Universal/navbarMobile.js` gains `closeMobileMenu()` — sets
`model.app.mobileMenuToggle = false` and removes `open` from `#mobile-menu`.
The router calls it from `renderRoute()`, so choosing a page closes the menu
and so does Back, which arrives on the same path.

`closeMobileMenu()` also sets `aria-expanded="false"` on the toggle button
when it carries the attribute — the class and the state say the same thing.

`renderRoute()` also compares the full `location.hash` it is about to render
with the one it last rendered (`lastResolvedHash`, already there) and, when
they differ, calls `window.scrollTo(0, 0)` after `updateView()`. The full
hash, not the route name: `#musicDetails?id=1` → `#musicDetails?id=2` is a
different album and starts at the top. A same-hash re-render — a library
filter, a form error — keeps its scroll position; that is what "every page is
independent" means in practice. The scroll is instant, not smooth, so there
is nothing for `prefers-reduced-motion` to object to. Nothing in the Model:
scroll is a View concern the Controller triggers, not state.

## 2 — Scan looks up on its own

The body of `lookupPressed()` in `Controller/Edit_Music_Details/lookup.js`
moves into `runLookup(digits)`. The button handler validates the field and
calls it; `scanPressed()` writes the digits into the field, runs
`barcodeTyped()` as today, and calls it too. One path, whichever way the
number arrived:

1. owned check — a barcode already on the shelf stops here with the owned
   note, focus on the note; a second scan of the same sleeve sends, exactly
   like a second press. A scan must not add a copy quietly.
2. look-ups pref — see § 4.
3. fetch, fill, cover — § 3.

Failure messages are the v0.4 ones (busy, no match, failed, timed out). The
Look up button stays: it is the path for a typed number and for iOS.

## 3 — Cover from the barcode

### What leaves the device

One more request, only after a match is picked (or the single match fills)
**and only when the cover field is empty**, the same fill-only-empty rule as
the text fields:

```
GET https://coverartarchive.org/release-group/<release-group id>/front-500
```

The archive answers with a redirect to where the file lives
(`archive.org`, and from there possibly an `*.archive.org` mirror). The exact
chain is the first task of the build plan: a probe from the dev server, written
down, because `connect-src` has to name every hop and I cannot verify that
from a spec. `img-src` does not change — the image is fetched as a blob and
becomes a data URL like a chosen photo, so it never loads as a remote image.

`connect-src` becomes `https://musicbrainz.org https://coverartarchive.org
https://archive.org https://*.archive.org` (adjusted to what the probe shows).
The dev-server replacement in `vite.config.js` keys on the `connect-src`
marker and throws when it no longer matches; the marker changes with the
header, in the same commit.

### Data

`collapseReleases()` in `Model/musicbrainz.js` keeps one more field per match:
`releaseGroupId`, the `release-group.id` of the first release in that
artist+title group. It is kept only when it is a UUID —
`/^[0-9a-f]{8}-[0-9a-f]{4}-[0-9a-f]{4}-[0-9a-f]{4}-[0-9a-f]{12}$/` — and is
`""` otherwise. The value comes from a third party and goes into a URL path;
missing and malformed are the same case: no cover request, the *no cover*
message, never a request built from it.

Nothing new is stored on the album. The cover arrives as `coverPreview`, goes
through `downscaleCover()` like a photo, and is written to IndexedDB by Save as
today. Cancelling the form drops it.

### The flow

`runLookup()` → match picked → text fields filled → if `coverPreview` is
empty and `releaseGroupId` is set, `fetchCover(releaseGroupId, signal)` in
`Model/musicbrainz.js` (same module: same project, same abort controller,
same 10 s timeout). The blob is trusted by its bytes, not its header: it goes
through the same magic-byte check `View/Universal/sniff.js` applies to a
chosen file, and anything over 2 MB is refused before decoding — `front-500`
is a request, not a guarantee. When the response arrives, `coverPreview` is
checked **again**: if the user chose a photo while the archive was slow, the
fetched image is dropped silently and no message is written — they already
have what they wanted.

Result states, each with its own key in both language files. The cover
result is **appended to the fill sentence** the live region already carries
(*Filled title and year. Cover added from the archive.*) — one write, one
announcement — rather than a second write a screen reader may talk over:

| Result | Message (EN) |
|---|---|
| 200, sniffed as an image, decoded | *Cover added from the Cover Art Archive. Change it below if it is the wrong pressing.* |
| 404 | *No cover in the archive for this album — add one below.* |
| 503 / 429 | *The cover archive is busy — try Look up again in a moment.* |
| network / timeout / too large / not an image | *The cover could not be fetched. The details were filled in; add a cover below.* |
| aborted (navigated away) | nothing — same as v0.4 |
| cover chosen meanwhile | nothing |

The text fields are already filled before the cover request starts, so a
cover failure never costs the metadata. The cover request is not retried on
its own; Look up again re-runs the whole thing and is honest about it.

`front-500` caps the download at a 500 px edge, well under the downscale
target, and keeps a slow connection from pulling a 3000 px scan.

### Consent

The consent dialog (v0.4 § 5) and the standing note under the field name both
hosts and say that a cover may be fetched for the album picked. The About
page's "what leaves the device" paragraph adds the second host. Nothing is
requested from the archive unless a MusicBrainz request has already been
agreed to — it is one consent, not two.

## 4 — The look-ups question after Create library

### Pref

`lookups` in `Model/prefs.js` gains a third value and a new default:

```
lookups: "unset" | "on" | "off"     default "unset"
```

Whitelist updated to match, and the blob gains `prefsVersion: 2`. A stored
blob without `prefsVersion` is v0.4's, and `readPrefs()` migrates it once on
the way in, then writes it back: `lookups: "on"` → `"unset"`, because that
yes was given for one host and v0.5 sends to two — they see the updated
dialog once, naming both, and answer again. `"off"` stays `"off"`: that was a
deliberate Profile choice and it still means no. A person who never answered
has no key and reads the default, `"unset"`.

### The question

`register.js`, after the library is written and before `navigate("homePage")`:
one `openDialog()` — title *Look up barcodes online?*, body naming both hosts
and what is sent, confirm *Yes, look them up*, cancel *No, keep it offline*.
Yes → `setPref("lookups", "on")`; the **No button** → `"off"`. Escape, Back
and any other dismissal leave it `"unset"`: someone who closes an unexpected
dialog to get their bearings has not answered, and the v0.4-style dialog
asks once on their first press. "No means no" holds — it needs a no. It is
asked here once because a library is created once.

### What `off` means

- `runLookup()` step 2: `"on"` → send; `"unset"` → the v0.4 dialog, then on
  yes send, on no return; `"off"` → set the status line to *Look-ups are off.
  Turn them on under Profile to fill in details from the barcode.* and return.
  The number stays in the field either way.
- Scan is unchanged in what it shows: the camera, the number, then the line
  above instead of a request.
- Profile keeps the toggle. Its two options stay *off* / *on*; picking either
  from `unset` is the same as answering the dialog.

## 5 — Stay unlocked on this device

### The threat, accepted

Today the AES key exists only in a module variable. Close the tab, reload,
lose the phone — the library is ciphertext. With this option on, **anyone who
holds the unlocked device can open the library until I log out.** That is the
whole trade, it is opt-in, and it is said in the Login form's help text, in
Profile next to the toggle, and in About. It does not weaken anything else:
the password is still never stored, backups are still encrypted, the key is
still non-extractable.

### What is stored

The non-extractable AES-GCM `CryptoKey` from `deriveKeys()` — a `CryptoKey`
is structured-cloneable, so IndexedDB can hold it, and *non-extractable* means
script can use it to decrypt but can never read its bytes. Not the verify key
(nothing to verify at boot), not the salt (it is in the envelope), not the
username, not the password. One record, `id: "session"`, in a new object
store `session` in the existing `spindle` database; `DB_VERSION` goes to 2
and `onupgradeneeded` creates the store when missing.

`Model/auth.js` gets three functions: `storeSessionKey(key)`,
`readSessionKey()`, `clearSessionKey()`. `Model/covers.js`'s `openDb()` is
shared, not copied.

### When it is written

`login()`, after the HMAC check passes and before `navigate("homePage")`, when
the Login form's checkbox is checked. The checkbox is `model.viewState.login.stay`
(default `false`), rendered by `View/Login/view.js` with its own label and a
one-line help text under it. Unchecked is the default every time the form
renders; the app does not remember the choice, the key is the choice.

The store can refuse: private mode, storage disabled, a WebKit that will not
clone a `CryptoKey`. Then the login still succeeds, `model.app.stayUnlocked`
stays `false`, and the auth message says *Logged in, but this device could
not be kept unlocked.* — a checked box that quietly did nothing would be the
lie this plan's standing rule exists to prevent.

### When it is read

`boot.js`, before the first route renders: `readSessionKey()`; if a key comes
back and an envelope exists, decrypt with it through the same unlock path
`login()` uses (the decrypt-and-normalize part of `login()` moves into
`unlockWith(encryptKey)` so the two paths cannot drift), then render Home. If
decrypting fails — the envelope was replaced by a backup from another device,
the store is corrupt — the key is cleared, the Login page renders with the
line *This device was unlocked, but the library has changed. Log in again.*
and nothing is thrown. A missing key is the normal case and is silent.

### When it is cleared

`clearSessionKey()` is called from every path that ends the session or changes
the key: `logout()`, change password, delete library, restore a backup, and
the Profile toggle set to off. Change password is the one that continues the
session, so it does not drop the choice: it clears the old key and, when
`model.app.stayUnlocked` is true, stores the new one in the same step. The
other three end the session and clear outright. The Profile toggle set to on while logged in
calls `storeSessionKey()` with the key already in memory — no password prompt,
it is the same key. The toggle shows `model.app.stayUnlocked`, which `boot.js`
sets from the store before the first render and every write to the store
updates — views render synchronously and IndexedDB does not, so the state has
to be mirrored, not read at render.

### Cross-tab

`storageSync.js` reacts to `localStorage` events only; IndexedDB has none.
The session store is read at boot only, so a second tab sees the key on its
next reload, which is enough. Logging out in one tab clears the key for both.

### What this does not do

It does not add an idle timer, a lock button or a PIN. Log out is the lock.
If a window or a timer is wanted later, it is a value on the same record and
a check in `boot.js`, nothing more.

## 7 — Verification

Before it is called done:

- **Menu:** open the mobile menu at 375 px, choose Library → `#mobile-menu`
  has no `open` class and `model.app.mobileMenuToggle` is `false`; Back →
  same.
- **Scroll:** on Library scrolled to 800 px, choose About → `scrollY` is 0;
  change a filter on Library scrolled to 800 px → `scrollY` unchanged.
- **Scan sends:** `openScanner` stubbed to resolve `"720642442524"`, `fetch`
  stubbed → `fetch` is called once with the MusicBrainz URL and no press.
- **Scan owned:** same stub with that barcode already on the shelf → `fetch`
  not called, focus on the owned note.
- **Cover fill:** fixture match with `releaseGroupId`, cover field empty,
  `fetch` answering a 1×1 PNG blob → `coverPreview` set, status
  `lookup.coverAdded`. Cover field already set → the second `fetch` never
  happens.
- **Cover states:** `fetch` answering 404 / 503 / a rejected promise / a
  `text/html` body → each of the four messages, and the text fields are
  filled in every case.
- **Release group missing or malformed:** match with `releaseGroupId: ""`,
  and a fixture whose `release-group.id` is `"../release/x"` → no request,
  `lookup.coverNone`.
- **Cover chosen meanwhile:** `fetch` stubbed to resolve after 1 s,
  `coverPreview` set during the wait → the fetched image is dropped, the
  preview is the user's, no cover message.
- **Blob sniff:** `fetch` answering `image/png` headers with an HTML body,
  and a 3 MB image → `lookup.coverFailed`, nothing decoded.
- **Abort:** navigate away during the cover request → aborted, next page's
  form untouched.
- **Pref:** `getPref("lookups")` with no stored key → `"unset"`; stored
  `"off"` → `"off"`; stored garbage → `"unset"`. A stored v0.4 blob
  `{lookups:"on"}` with no `prefsVersion` → `"unset"`, and the blob written
  back carries `prefsVersion: 2`; the same with `"off"` → `"off"`.
- **Question:** create a library with the dialog resolving `true` → pref
  `"on"`; the No button → `"off"`; Escape → still `"unset"`; then a Scan with
  `"off"` → no `fetch`, status `lookup.off`.
- **Store refused:** `storeSessionKey` stubbed to reject → login succeeds,
  `model.app.stayUnlocked` is `false`, auth message `login.stayFailed`.
- **Change password with the option on:** the store holds a key that
  decrypts the re-encrypted envelope; with the option off, the store is
  empty.
- **Stay unlocked:** log in with the box checked → a record exists in the
  `session` store; reload → Home renders without Login and the library is
  readable; log out → record gone, reload → Welcome (the app's pre-existing
  unauthenticated bounce, unrelated to this box). Log in unchecked → no
  record.
- **Key rejected:** store a key, replace the envelope with one encrypted under
  another password, reload → Login with `login.sessionStale`, record gone,
  no uncaught error in the console.
- **Every clearing path:** change password, delete library, restore backup,
  Profile toggle off — each leaves the store empty.
- **CSP:** one real cover fetch from the dev server succeeds end to end;
  `curl -I` on the deployed URL shows the widened `connect-src`.
- **Layout:** the Login checkbox row, the Profile stay row and the new status
  lines measured at 375 / 768 / 1280 in both languages, 44×44 targets, zero
  horizontal overflow.
- **Done on the phone (2026-09-22).** The camera opens from Scan, so
  `camera=(self)` does the job it was widened for. I scanned two real CDs;
  Eminem – Recovery filled its fields and pulled its cover from the archive a
  few seconds later. Switching to another app and coming back with the box
  checked leaves me on Home. The cover field opens the camera as designed.
- **Not verified on iOS, and staying that way for now.** I do not have access
  to an iPhone, so whether the `CryptoKey` survives a round trip through
  IndexedDB on Safari is untested. The report says "not verified on iOS".
- **One cover did not arrive.** Eminem – Revival filled its fields and showed
  no cover. Its release group does carry a front cover: `front-500` is 84 KB,
  well under the 2 MB cap, and it fetches 200 from the live origin under the
  deployed CSP. So the cause is whichever of `lookup.coverNone`,
  `lookup.coverBusy` or `lookup.coverFailed` the status line showed, and a
  transient 503 from the archive is the likeliest of the three. Those three
  messages exist precisely so this is answerable next time.
- **Two things the pass sent to v0.6.** The Scan button belongs at the top of
  Add album rather than third in the barcode row, and the cover field wants
  automatic cropping — § 8 ruled cropping out for v0.5 on the grounds that it
  would need a manual adjust step anyway, and the phone pass says the manual
  option is a fine fallback rather than a replacement.

## 8 — Not in v0.5

- Camera capture with automatic sleeve cropping. Ruled out 2026-09-18 as
  unreliable enough to need a manual adjust step anyway; the plain camera on
  the cover field covers the gap.
- Any image recognition of a sleeve.
- An idle timer, PIN or lock button — § 5 says why not yet.
- `dateAdded`, `pushState`, the scanner/dialog exit-handling extraction —
  still deferred, reasons unchanged from `docs/v0.3-plan.md` § 8 and
