# Spindle v0.8: plan

Spec of record for v0.8. v0.7.1 is closed: removing a genre or a location no
longer shifts the labels on other albums, and the album card rows wrap the way
I sorted them on 2026-09-30.

v0.8 straightens covers. A sleeve photographed on a table is often a few
degrees off, and one shot from above often comes out sideways, because a
phone held flat cannot tell which way is up. The crop dialog gets a button
that turns the photo a quarter, and a slider and a two-finger twist that
straighten it by up to 45 degrees. The square still never shows a gap.

**Status:** designed 2026-10-07. Stress test pending.

**All decisions are made (2026-10-07).** They are in § 0.

---

## 0. Decisions

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which crooked photos does v0.8 fix? | **A sleeve turned a few degrees, and a photo that came out sideways or upside down.** A sleeve shot from an angle (a trapezoid) is out: it needs perspective correction, which no canvas API does, and four corner handles, which v0.7 § 0 decision 4 rejected. |
| 2 | How do I turn and straighten? | **A button turns 90° clockwise. A slider straightens from −45° to 45°. Two fingers twist on a phone.** A quarter turn plus a tilt covers every angle, so the tilt never needs more than 45°. The slider is the path for a keyboard, a mouse and a screen reader; the twist is the quick one on a phone. |
| 3 | Does the dialog guess the angle? | **No. It always opens at 0°.** A crooked guess is worse than a straight one, and the background-colour guess from v0.7 stays as it is. |
| 4 | How is the rotation built? | **The frame turns, not the photo.** The frame gains a centre, a quarter and a tilt; the stage and the saved cover are drawn through the same rotation. Baking each angle into a new bitmap was rejected: its empty corners would get into the square, and every slider move would redraw a photo up to 4096 px. |
| 5 | What happens to the zoom when I tilt? | **The square keeps its zoom relative to the largest square that fits.** Tilting zooms in, because a tilted square needs more photo around it. Straightening again zooms back out to where I was, unless the 200 px floor stopped it on the way. A quarter turn never zooms. |
| 6 | Does the trackpad pinch get fixed? | **Measured first, fixed only if slow.** Task 1 is me pinching on my trackpad in the live dialog. Sluggish: the `ctrlKey` multiplier in § 4 goes in, with one Node test. Fine: the task closes with no code. |

v0.7 § 0 decision 5 still holds: only the cropped result is kept, and changing
a crop later means picking the photo again.

### The shape

A frame was `{ x, y, side }`, an upright square in the photo's pixels. It
becomes `{ cx, cy, side, quarter, tilt }`:

- `cx`, `cy`: the square's centre in the photo's upright pixels.
- `side`: the square's side in photo pixels.
- `quarter`: 0 to 3, the number of 90° turns clockwise.
- `tilt`: −45 to 45 degrees, in 0.5° steps.

The photo's angle on screen is `θ = quarter × 90 + tilt`, clockwise. I keep
`quarter` and `tilt` apart because the slider shows the tilt only and the
button changes the quarter only.

`guessSquare` and `centreSquare` still return `{ x, y, side }`. A new
`frameFromSquare(square)` turns that into a frame with `quarter` and `tilt` at
0, so the guess does not change.

### Screen and photo

The square stays upright on screen and the photo turns under it. A point `s`
on the stage, measured from the stage's centre in photo pixels, is the photo
point

```
p = c + R(−θ) · s
```

where `c` is `(cx, cy)` and `R(φ)` rotates by `φ` clockwise in y-down
coordinates. This one mapping is used for drawing, for the pointer and for
saving, so the three cannot disagree.

### The no-gap rule

A square tilted by `θ` lies inside the photo exactly when its bounding box
does, because the photo is an upright rectangle and the square's corners are
what touch the box. The box's half-width is

```
e = side × k / 2,   k = |cos θ| + |sin θ|
```

`k` is 1 at 0° and 90°, and √2 at 45°. It depends only on the tilt: a quarter
turn swaps the sine and the cosine, and the sum stays the same.

- **`EDGE_INSET`.** When the tilt is not 0, the square keeps 1 photo pixel away
  from the edge. Smoothing at the exact border blends in the transparent
  pixels outside the photo, which would leave a faint see-through fringe along
  the edge of a saved cover. At tilt 0 the inset is 0, so an untilted crop is
  v0.7's crop to the pixel.
- **`sideLimits(photo, frame)`.** The largest side is
  `(min(w, h) − 2 × inset) / k`. The smallest is v0.7's: a quarter of the
  shorter side, never under 200 px, and never above the largest. Both are in
  photo pixels, because too little detail is a matter of photo pixels.
- **`clampFrame(frame, photo)`.** Clamps the side, then the centre to
  `[e + inset, w − e − inset]` across and `[e + inset, h − e − inset]` down.
  Tilt is clamped to ±45 and `quarter` taken modulo 4.

### Moving the frame

One function does the work:

```
transformAt(frame, photo, side, tilt, px, py)
```

It applies a new side and tilt while the photo point `(px, py)` stays on the
same spot of the screen, then clamps. The new centre is

```
c′ = p − (side′ / side) · R(θ − θ′) · (p − c)
```

Everything else is built on it:

- **Zoom** (wheel, pinch, the − and + buttons, the + and - keys): a new side at
  the same tilt. This is v0.7's `zoomAt`, generalised; `zoomBy` keeps its name
  and its pivot at the square's centre.
- **`setTilt(frame, photo, tilt, px, py)`** (slider, twist): the side becomes
  `side × k(old) / k(new)` (decision 5). The slider pivots on the square's
  centre. The twist pivots on the midpoint between the fingers and applies the
  pinch ratio in the same step, so one gesture zooms and turns at once.
- **`panBy(frame, photo, dx, dy)`** (drag, arrow keys): the screen movement is
  turned into photo directions first, `c′ = c − R(−θ) · (dx, dy)`. A drag to
  the right moves the photo right on screen at any angle, and so does the
  right arrow key.
- **`turnQuarter(frame, photo)`**: `quarter + 1`, centre and side unchanged,
  then clamped. `k` does not change, so a turn never zooms; the clamp only
  matters when the centre sat near an edge that is now the short one.
- `canZoomIn`, `canZoomOut` and `keyStep` read the limits for the current tilt.

### The twist

A pure helper, `twistTilt(startTilt, turned)`, holds the two rules, so the
Node suite can test them:

- **A dead zone of `TWIST_DEAD_ZONE` (10°) per gesture.** Every real pinch
  turns the fingers a little, and without a threshold every zoom would leave
  the cover a degree or two crooked. Until the fingers have turned 10° in
  this gesture, the tilt stays at `startTilt`. Past it, the tilt follows the
  fingers 1:1, counted from the moment it engaged, so the photo does not jump
  by 10° when it starts.
- **A snap to 0° within `TWIST_SNAP` (1°) when the gesture ends.** Two fingers
  cannot land exactly straight, and straight is what I am twisting towards
  most of the time. The slider and the keys never snap, so 0.5° is still
  reachable.

The angle between the fingers comes from `atan2`, with the jump at ±180°
unwrapped, so a twist across that line is not a sudden full turn. A twist that
reaches ±45° stops there. It never wraps into the next quarter; only the
button changes quarters. A third finger is ignored, as in v0.7.

## 2. The dialog

### Layout, phone first

Top to bottom: the title, the stage, the **straighten row**, the hint, the
actions row.

- **Straighten row.** A visible `<label>` (`crop.straighten`), a native
  `<input type="range">` from −45 to 45 with step 0.5, and an `<output>` with
  the value, formatted for the language: "3.5°" in English, "3,5°" in
  Norwegian. `aria-valuetext` reads the same number in words
  (`crop.degreesSpoken`), so a screen reader says "3.5 degrees" and not "3.5".
  The native slider brings arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, Home and End,
  touch and mouse without code of mine.
- **Actions row.** `[−] [+] [⟳]` on the left, `[Cancel] [Use]` on the right.
  The turn button is the new `rotate` icon with `crop.rotate` as its
  `aria-label`. It is a trusted constant from `icons.js`, the one place this
  dialog sets markup rather than `textContent`. When the row does not fit it
  wraps as in v0.7: the tool group, then Cancel and Use, never a horizontal
  scroll.
- **One height, one bottom edge.** The slider row, the tools and the answer
  buttons are 44 px tall, and the controls on each row share one bottom edge.
  Measured, not assumed (§ 5).
- **The stage's height budget.** The stage is
  `min(100%, max(12rem, calc(100dvh − 12rem)))` today. The 12rem grows by the
  straighten row's height, so on a phone the title and Use still fit without
  scrolling. The 12rem floor and the scrolling fallback for 200 % text stay.

### The grid

Straight cannot be judged against nothing, so a rule-of-thirds grid is drawn
over the photo while the tilt is changing: on slider input and during an
engaged twist. It fades out `GRID_LINGER` (500 ms) after the last change.
Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it goes at once instead of fading.
The lines are thin and light with a dark edge, so they show on a white sleeve
and on a black one. It is drawn on the canvas, so the DOM and the screen
reader see nothing new.

### What stays as it is

The fixed square with the photo moving under it, focus starting on the stage,
the stage's keys (arrows move, + and - zoom, Enter is Use), Escape and Back as
Cancel, no backdrop dismiss, one draw per animation frame, the 4096 px preview
copy, and the once-only answer. The turn button and the slider are disabled
with the others once I have answered.

## 3. Saving

`encodeCover(bitmap, frame)` draws the square to a canvas of
`min(700, side)` px, never enlarged, through the same rotation as the stage:
move to the canvas centre, rotate by `θ`, scale by `output / side`, draw the
photo offset by `(−cx, −cy)`. WebP at 0.82 with the PNG fallback, EXIF and GPS
dropped by the re-encode, exactly as in v0.7.

- **No turn, no tilt.** The v0.7 path runs as it is,
  `drawImage(sx, sy, sw, sh, …)` with `x = cx − side / 2`. An untouched crop
  saves the same bytes as v0.7.
- **Quarter turns without tilt.** The offsets are rounded to whole pixels and
  the angle is an exact multiple of 90°, so a turn moves pixels without
  blurring them.
- **Tilt.** `imageSmoothingQuality = "high"`. The `EDGE_INSET` keeps every
  sampled pixel inside the photo, so the corners of the cover are opaque.

`editMusic.js` passes `frameFromSquare(guess)` to `cropPhoto` and the frame it
returns to `encodeCover`. Decode, the guess, Cancel, the refusal of a photo
that fails to decode or encode, and the look-up path through `downscaleCover`
do not change.

## 4. The trackpad pinch (task 1)

A trackpad pinch arrives as `wheel` with `ctrlKey` and small deltas, about
`exp(−deltaY / 100)` of scale per event. `wheelFactor()` counts 100 px as one
notch, so a pinch may zoom about ten times slower than the gesture asks for.
Nobody has measured it.

1. I open the crop dialog on the deployed site with a laptop trackpad and
   pinch.
2. **Fine:** the task closes and this section says so.
3. **Sluggish:** `wheelFactor(deltaY, deltaMode, ctrlKey)` multiplies the
   notches by `PINCH_GAIN` (10) when `ctrlKey` is set, before the one-notch
   clamp. One Node test: a pinch of `deltaY = 10` with `ctrlKey` zooms the same
   as a wheel notch. The mouse wheel is untouched.

## 7. Not in v0.8

- Perspective correction for a sleeve shot from an angle (decision 1).
- Guessing the angle (decision 3).
- Mirroring or flipping a photo. A photo is not mirrored by a phone camera
  the way it can be turned.
- A twist on a trackpad. Browsers expose that gesture only through Safari's
  non-standard events; the slider is the desktop path.
- Keeping the original photo to re-crop later (v0.7 decision 5).
- iOS verification: a friend tests on an iPhone once Spindle is finalized.

## 8. Traps carried in

- **Implementer subagents run on Sonnet or better.** A cheaper model rewrote
  `Model/i18n/en.js` from 317 lines to 44 in v0.5 and reported success; the
  Node suite does not cover the string tables.
- **The browser pane cannot pinch or twist.** Two-pointer gestures are proven
  by the `cropFrame.js` tests and on my phone.
- **A hidden pane lays out at 0 px wide.** Resize before measuring anything.
- **Vite HMR splits the module graph.** Restart the dev server before driving
  the model from `javascript_tool`.
- **A pane script re-queries `#music-cover` after every pick.** Any render
  replaces the file input.
- **A hidden pane defers a `<dialog>`'s `close` event.** Wait for `close`, or
  dispatch it by hand, before judging a double answer.
